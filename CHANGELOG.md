# Changelog

What changed for visitors, newest first. The page shows the entries a returning
visitor hasn't seen yet, so keep each one short and plain.

## 1.9.1 · 2026-11-14 · Contact

- "? WHAT IS THIS" now ends with a contact address: dirk@primbs.ca.

## 1.9.0 · 2026-11-13 · Back to basics

- Norbert thinks the way it did in 1.1 again. The changes from 1.2 to 1.8 (reply sizes, the Feeler and mood, recruiting voices, self-discovery, curiosity, the voice's moods) added up to replies that were less clear, so they are out until each can be tried on its own.
- Kept: Bluetooth headsets no longer swallow the first words; ■ stop silences the whole reply; export/import of voice settings; the long-term memory in its own window (◉ MEMORY).

## 1.8.1 · 2026-11-13 · Said so you can follow it

- Fixed: replies could turn into dense, riddling shorthand when the voices kept swapping in new ideas. Norbert now writes for someone hearing it once: plain words, one idea told well, no leftovers from its inner discussion. A reply that can't be followed is sent back.
- Once a draft has gone through a few versions it settles: from then on only what is wrong or unclear gets fixed.

## 1.8.0 · 2026-11-13 · Curious about something

- Norbert acts on how it feels. When something makes it curious, it says so and, in a conversation, asks you about it.
- What it is curious about, and what a conversation shows about it (what it enjoys, how it reacts), is remembered as something it found out about itself, so its personality can actually grow.
- Its feelings move more: it reports how each moment feels rather than repeating its mood, and the spoken reply's energy follows how stirred up it is.
- The Inventor joins conversations; nobody sends the notebook keeper errands any more.

## 1.7.0 · 2026-11-13 · Something to say

- In a conversation Norbert brings something of its own (a reaction, a thought, sometimes a question back) instead of handing your words back to you. Replies that only repeat what you said, or state the obvious, are sent back.
- The critic stops sanding off lively details over harmless assumptions.

## 1.6.0 · 2026-11-13 · Speaking up uninvited

- The Storyteller thinks about every question again: even a sum or a fact is easier to take in with one clear example.
- Voices that weren't called for a question can still speak up. Once the first draft is written, each resting voice takes one look and joins if it has something that clearly makes the answer better. The stream shows who spoke up uninvited.

## 1.5.0 · 2026-11-13 · A mood of its own

- Norbert has a mood. A new voice, the Feeler, notices how a question lands (how the asker seems to feel, how heavy or light the subject is) and how it feels about the answer. Its reactions move Norbert's mood, which colours the tone of the answer and how it is spoken. The mood is shown at the top and carries over to the next conversation, drifting back to curious over a few hours.
- Not every voice thinks about everything any more. The Listener hears first and says what kind of question it is, and only the voices it needs join in; the others rest (shown faded on the brain) unless someone calls on them. Simple questions get answered with less fuss.
- The Coordinator keeps its notebook but no longer tells the others what to do.
- Fixed: the first words of the spoken reply could be swallowed by Bluetooth headsets waking up. A short, inaudible lead-in now wakes the audio first.

## 1.4.0 · 2026-11-13 · Finding out who it is

- Norbert starts out knowing very little about what it is, where it is or what it's for, and says so instead of guessing.
- What it finds out about itself in conversation (told, noticed or worked out) is remembered, and it tells you what it knows rather than assuming.
- A discovery that holds up in three separate conversations is weighed carefully, and may become part of who Norbert is: a core belief that never fades. Its personality grows from what it finds out.
- The long-term memory has its own window (◉ MEMORY at the top, or click the memory count): who Norbert is, what it has found out about itself, what it has learned about you, and what it remembers.

## 1.3.0 · 2026-11-13 · Knowing when to stop

- Norbert decides up front how long a reply should be: a sentence, a few sentences, a paragraph or a long answer. A quick question gets a quick answer, and in a conversation it says less, as people do.
- The voices that check the answer now also ask what can go, and a draft far over its size is sent back to be cut.
- Norbert learns how much you like to hear. Stop the spoken reply partway, or ask for "shorter" or "tell me more", and it remembers. The stream shows what it learned.
- The spoken reply's mood is clearer: the voice picks a feeling, an energy and a pace for each reply, and says why it feels that way.

## 1.2.0 · 2026-11-12 · A voice with feelings

- Norbert's spoken reply has a mood now. The voice that speaks hears how the thinking went (how interested Norbert was, whether the answer passed its own check first time) and says how the reply should sound: delighted, wry, a little unsure… The stream shows it under the spoken reply.
- Configure voices can export all the voices' settings to a file and import such a file again, to try different setups and switch back.

## 1.1.1 · 2026-11-11

- Fixed: ■ stop on the spoken reply stopped only the current sentence; the rest of the reply kept coming. Now it stops the whole reply, and stopping the session silences it too.

## 1.1.0 · 2026-11-11 · Punchier stories

- Norbert likes to keep a story short and punchy, and to end it on a punchline or a twist.
- A story no longer has to be told in the first person just because Norbert is telling it.
- Fixed: drafts could be cut off mid-sentence when a model thought longer than it was allowed to.
- New models for most voices, chosen by testing each voice's job on 16 models: Kimi K3.1 Pro writes the drafts now, and the voices that often held things up run on faster models.
- Fixed: wasted requests to models that insist on thinking, and voices failing on models that don't take a temperature.

## 1.0.0 · 2026-11-11 · Norbert

- The brain has a name now: **Norbert**. A few core beliefs about who Norbert is (the name, a temperament, saying "I don't know" rather than guessing) never fade from memory.
- Claim Norbert is someone else and the brain won't simply take it on. It thinks the claim over, and changes a core belief only if it stays convinced across more than one conversation.
- A version number next to the title, and this changelog. Click the version to read it again.

## 0.4.0 · 2026-11-11 · Threads

- The brain remembers what it was asked along with what it replied, so a follow-up question picks up where the conversation left off.
- Memories are linked into threads: "I said this after you asked that".

## 0.3.0 · 2026-11-11 · Long-term memory

- A long-term memory that keeps what was said, what the brain came to believe and what it found interesting. Memories that get used grow stronger; the rest fade, and the weakest are forgotten.
- ⚙ Settings lists the memories and can export or import everything the app keeps (except your OpenRouter key).

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-11 · Sign in with OpenRouter

- **Sign in with OpenRouter** instead of pasting a key.
- The in-browser models are gone: they were too small to hold a conversation together.
- The Voice speaks only real answers, never a placeholder or a rejected draft.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-10

- The first public version: a brain in a tank that thinks in inner speech, with five demo sessions to replay.
