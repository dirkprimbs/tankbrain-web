# Changelog

What changed for visitors, newest first. The page shows the entries a returning
visitor hasn't seen yet, so keep each one short and plain.

## 1.0.0 · 2026-11-11 · Norbert

- The brain has a name now: **Norbert**. A few core beliefs about who Norbert is (the name, a temperament, saying "I don't know" rather than guessing) never fade from memory.
- Claim Norbert is someone else and the brain won't simply take it on. It thinks the claim over, and changes a core belief only if it stays convinced across more than one conversation.
- A version number next to the title, and this changelog. Click the version to read it again.

## 0.4.0 · 2026-11-11 · Threads

- The brain remembers what it was asked along with what it replied, so a follow-up question picks up where the conversation left off.
- Memories are linked into threads: "I said this after you asked that".

## 0.3.0 · 2026-11-11 · Long-term memory

- A long-term memory that keeps what was said, what the brain came to believe and what it found interesting. Memories that get used grow stronger; the rest fade, and the weakest are forgotten.
- ⚙ Settings lists the memories and can export or import everything the app keeps (except your OpenRouter key).

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-11 · Sign in with OpenRouter

- **Sign in with OpenRouter** instead of pasting a key.
- The in-browser models are gone: they were too small to hold a conversation together.
- The Voice speaks only real answers, never a placeholder or a rejected draft.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-10

- The first public version: a brain in a tank that thinks in inner speech, with five demo sessions to replay.
